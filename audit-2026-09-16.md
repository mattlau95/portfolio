## Executive summary

Five media figures were added to the Kumon case study (race video, annotated worksheet, three popup states, four status bars, plus a commented-out bulk-run placeholder). The race figure runs on the site's wide track at 1.4× the text column on desktop and swaps to a stacked portrait cut under 700px. The `70+ students tracked weekly` metric was removed.

The page ships clean on accessibility: **0 axe violations, 0 pa11y issues, Lighthouse Accessibility 100, Best Practices 100** on both presets. No horizontal scroll at any tested width, the Pause/Play button is the third tab stop with a visible focus ring, and the clip starts paused under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Three of the four Core Web Vitals now pass.** Mobile LCP moved from 2.9s to **1.96s** and desktop CLS from 0.144 to **0.051**, both crossing under threshold — dropping one metric row shortened the header enough to cut the font-swap reflow that drives both. Mobile CLS is still over at **0.171**, but it is better than the 0.24 baseline and the cause is the same site-wide font-swap issue deferred since 2026-09-15. **0 blockers, 1 important, 5 polish.**

**Notes (not scored as findings)**

- **`color-contrast` incomplete ×16** — axe cannot resolve the background behind text because `.spotlight` paints a radial gradient over the page. Site-wide, not specific to this change: Collette reports 6, Ollae 26, all with 0 actual violations.
- **`video-caption` incomplete ×1** — axe flags every `<video>` without a `<track kind="captions">`. This clip has **no audio track** (per the media README), so WCAG 1.2.2 does not apply; the `aria-label` and the figcaption carry the text alternative. No action.
- **Localhost artifacts:** the 301 redirect from `…​.html`, `cache-insight` and `render-blocking-insight` all appear on the baseline run and in the June and September audits. Not page-specific.
- **A latent bug in the figure system was found and fixed while doing this.** `.figure--wide` set `width` but not `max-width`, so combining it with `.media-figure` (which caps at `max-width: 100%`) silently clamped the track straight back to the column — the wide hero rendered at 1.0×. Collette never hit it because `.hero-figure` carries no `max-width`. `.figure--wide` now sets `max-width: none`; Collette re-verified unchanged at 969px.

## Content flags — raised, not changed

Neither is an audit finding; both are copy questions the media now makes visible to a reader.

1. **The "multi-day" framing sits oddly next to the clip.** The page says multi-day in six places (meta description, og:description, deck, the `multi-day` metric value, Overview, Outcome). The race clip shows one set taking 50.7s by hand; the media README does the arithmetic — 200 sets is under three hours of clicking. A reader who watches the clip can do that math. The README suggests either timing a few heavier sets by hand, or describing the cost as attention split across a workday rather than elapsed days.
2. **"default: 14 days" vs the screenshot's 10.** The Design paragraph says the gradebook bulk mode defaults to a 14-day threshold; `popup-gradebook.webp` shows "Clear worksheets older than **10** days", and the alt text (used verbatim from the embed) says 10. One of the two needs to move.
3. **`70+` still appears twice in body copy** — "a centre tracking 70+ active students" (The problem) and "a 70+ student roster" (Outcome) — now that the metric tile is gone. Left as is per the no-body-copy rule; flagging in case removing the tile was meant to retire the claim entirely.
