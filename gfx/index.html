About this archive

Before design engineering, I spent five years as a full-time and freelance graphic designer: e-commerce product imagery for Amazon, on-air broadcast graphics for live TV news, motion and social content for a professional soccer team, and brand work for a financial-literacy nonprofit.

The throughline across all four: building a template once and reusing it inside someone else's brand guidelines, rather than designing one-off pieces. A composite system restyled across 100+ Amazon SKUs and several sub-brands. A packaging structure reused across a 16-book curriculum. A broadcast graphic template reused across daily live segments. A lineup animation reused every home game. Same system, different inputs.

This archive is unlisted — it's not linked from my main portfolio, and it isn't indexed by search engines. It exists for anyone who has the link.
