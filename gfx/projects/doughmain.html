Overview

Between winter 2017 and spring 2018, I volunteered as a digital designer for the Doughmain Financial Literacy Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that creates financial literacy educational materials for high school students. The work ranged across animated social media GIFs, packaging for a set of booklets, and presentation slides. Below is a glimpse of the projects I'm most proud of from my time there.

Giving Tuesday GIFs

Doughmain already had an established visual identity by the time I joined, so these ran inside their existing colors, type, and mascot illustration rather than introducing a new look.

FitKit booklet packaging

Doughmain's curriculum, the "FitKit," consisted of 16+ spiral-bound notebooks, and I was tasked with designing a package for it. Below is the design: a roll-end box with a locking cover and a magazine holder inside — one structural template, sized once and reused across all 16+ booklets rather than redesigned per booklet.
