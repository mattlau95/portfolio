Overview

During the 2019 season of the NWSL (National Women's Soccer League), I interned at Sky Blue FC, now known as Gotham FC. It was an amazing experience — I assisted the team's social media manager, creating social media graphics, promotional videos, 2D animations, and anything else that needed a hand.

Because the digital media department had a small budget and a small team, I wore a lot of hats as an intern. That meant real-world hours across Adobe Creative Suite, especially Photoshop, Premiere Pro, and After Effects. A sample of that work is below.

Red Bull Arena | Starting lineup animation

Sky Blue FC, now Gotham FC, played their first two official home games of 2019 at Red Bull Arena (Harrison, NJ). Their previous venue was much smaller and didn't even have a video board. On short notice, I created content for the new video board and ribbon screens available at the new arena.

Starting lineup animation

I used Photoshop to crop out player portraits and After Effects to animate the screens that introduced the home team's starting lineup. The animation itself was built as a template — team colors, crest, and motion locked in — so each home game just meant swapping in that match's portraits and names rather than re-animating from zero.
