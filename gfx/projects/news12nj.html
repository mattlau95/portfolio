Overview

Some of the most exciting moments of my career so far took place while working as a graphic artist for the local news network News 12 New Jersey. I was assigned to create on-air graphics and animated maps for live newscasts during various on-call shifts, either 4:00am–1:00pm or 2:00pm–11:00pm. Working for a live television news channel meant always being prepared to do things last-minute — a director or producer could request something at any time. Below is a small sample of my work, all made for News 12 NJ and broadcast on live TV.

Fullscreen monitors

I happened to work a shift the morning after LeBron James decided he was going to the Lakers, so I got to Photoshop the jersey onto him for the broadcast.

Bullet points

A producer asked me to build a package of graphics showing the official FBI document (scanned) in the background. I took inspiration from how John Oliver's graphics team shows quotes over his shoulder on Last Week Tonight. I built it as a template — the document backdrop and bullet-point frame stayed fixed, and different producers dropped in new quotes for different stories — which is what made it usable same-day on live TV instead of a one-off graphic for a single segment.
