Between Winter of 2017 and Spring of 2018, I volunteered as a digital designer for Doughmain Financial Literacy Foundation. They are a 501c3 non-profit organization that creates financial literacy educational materials for high school students. I worked on all sorts of digital design projects, including animated social media GIFs, packaging for a set of booklets, and powerpoint slides. Below is a glimpse of some of the projects I'm most proud of during my time with Doughmain!
